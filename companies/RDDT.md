# Reddit -- An Exceptional Growth Story Priced at My Base Case, With No Margin of Safety

Reddit runs more than a hundred thousand interest communities where people ask and answer questions, and earns most of its revenue from advertising shown beside those conversations.[1][3]
Revenue grew more than 60% for eight consecutive quarters, and the business now earns about 31% net margins with almost no capital.[2][3]
At the October 15, 2026 close the price equals my Base case, which already assumes about 25% annual owner-earnings growth for five years; the margin of safety is zero.
**Thesis status:** Thesis supported for the business, not for the price. **Confidence:** Low. **Valuation status:** Completed; Base value $163 per share against a $159.36 price.

**Identity and basis:** Reddit, Inc. (Delaware), Class A common stock RDDT on the NYSE; Class B carries ten votes per share, and Huffman controls about 76% of the vote through proxies; no ADR.[1][4]
Reviewed 2026-10-15 by Neo; information cutoff as in frontmatter.
US GAAP consolidated statements, December year-end; amounts in USD millions unless stated per share or in percent.[1]
**History covered:** 2022-2025 annual statements, the six months to June 30, 2026; listed since its March 2024 IPO, so no public downturn record exists.[1][2]

**Summary:** Reddit is a large, human-written knowledge base that monetizes attention through ads and, increasingly, licenses its content to AI companies.[1]
The right measure is operating income after stock pay, taxed at a normal rate, because the company has paid almost no tax so far and stock pay is a real owner cost.[1][2]
There is no downturn record: the company has been public for under three years, during a period of very rapid growth.[1]
The competence limit is the AI question, which cuts both ways and is unresolved.

## Moat

**Coverage: Assessed.** Perimeter: the whole platform, because communities, content and ads are one product.[1]

| Mechanism | Present / Absent / Unknown | Answer / evidence | Contrary evidence | Confidence |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Switching costs | Absent | Readers pay nothing and can leave freely. | Moderators and long-time posters have reputations they would lose. | Medium |
| Network effects | Present | Each community's value grows with contributors; more than 22 billion comments by December 2025.[1] | Logged-in users grew only 7% while logged-out users grew 27% in Q2 2026.[5] | Medium |
| Brand, patents or licenses | Present | The 10-K says Reddit is one of the most cited domains in AI across all models.[1] | AI models can answer from Reddit content without sending users to Reddit.[1] | Medium |
| Structural cost advantage | Present | Users create the content for free; gross margin 91.3%.[3] | Volunteer moderators can withdraw effort. | Medium |
| Efficient scale | Absent | Many platforms compete for the same attention. | None. | High |
| Scale economies shared | Absent | No evidence. | None. | Low |

**Summary:** The defense is a network of communities and a twenty-year archive of human answers that would be very hard to recreate.[1]
Its weakness is that much of the traffic arrives logged out through search, which Reddit does not control.[1][5]
The same content that makes Reddit valuable to AI companies lets AI answer questions without a visit.

| Force / question | Pressure | Evidence-based answer | Confidence |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Rivalry: can rivals compete away profit through price or capacity? | High | Google, Meta, TikTok and others compete for ad budgets.[1] | High |
| Entry: what prevents a funded newcomer from winning customers? | Low | A new community platform starts without the archive or the communities. | Medium |
| Suppliers: can essential providers capture the margin? | High | Search engines and app stores control much of the traffic.[1] | Medium |
| Buyers: can concentrated or price-sensitive customers force concessions? | Medium | Advertisers measure returns closely; Reddit must prove them.[1] | Medium |
| Substitutes: can another solution meet the need more cheaply or better? | High | AI tools trained on Reddit content can answer instead of a visit.[1] | Medium |

**Summary:** Supplier power and substitutes are the strongest pressures, and both come from the same companies: Google and the AI labs.[1]
Reddit's answer is to sell its content to them and grow logged-in use, but logged-in growth of 7% shows the second part is slow.[5]
The network is real; its toll gate is not yet secure.

| Protection /5 | Economics /5 | Durability /5 | Trend /5 | Class / trend | Confidence | Decisive evidence / limitation | Threat / reassessment trigger |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 3 | 3 | 2 | 4 | **Weak / strengthening** | Low | One full profitable year (2025); 91% gross margin and 31% net margin in Q2 2026.[1][3] | Logged-out traffic falling, or ARPU growth stalling. |

**Summary:** Economic proof is only 3 because the company has been profitable for about six quarters, too short to show durable returns.[1][2]
Durability is 2 because the traffic source sits with search and AI companies that are also competitors.[1]
Several more years of rising logged-in users and margins would raise the classification.

**Summary:** The weakest assumption is that AI search keeps sending people to Reddit rather than answering from Reddit's content.[1]
Buffett's four filters: understandable, economics excellent but young, adequate management under founder control, and no margin of safety.
The next evidence is Q3 2026 revenue against the 860-870 guide and the trend in logged-in users.[3][5]

### Final Verdict

Reddit is a remarkable growth business with a weak but strengthening moat, adequate management under founder control and resilient finances.[1][2][4]
At the NYSE close of $159.36 on 2026-10-15, Bear / Base / Bull earnings-proxy values of $65 / $163 / $415 put the price 2.4% below Base estimated intrinsic value (derived from the Valuation table; price from [8]).
My judgment is fairly valued relative to Base, with Low confidence: the price already pays for about 25% annual growth for five years, so there is no margin of safety, only an option on the Bull case.
A sustained fall in logged-out traffic or stalling ARPU would invalidate the Base case; a price near $114 would give a 30% margin.
