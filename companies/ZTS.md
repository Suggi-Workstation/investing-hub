# Zoetis -- The Leader in Animal Medicine, Priced as if US Pet Sales Never Recover

Zoetis is the world's largest animal health company, selling medicines, vaccines and diagnostics for pets and livestock.[1]
Revenue grew from 4,888 in 2016 to 9,467 in 2025, but in 2026 US companion animal sales fell, and full-year guidance moved from organic growth of 3% to 5% to a decline of 3% to 1%.[1][3][4][7]
At $78.42 the shares trade at about 13 times guided 2026 adjusted EPS (derived), close to my Base value.
**Thesis status:** Thesis not supported at the current price. **Confidence:** Medium. **Valuation status:** Completed; Base value $71 per share against a $78.42 price.

**Identity and basis:** Zoetis Inc. (Delaware), common stock ZTS on the NYSE; one class; 413,223,602 shares outstanding on July 31, 2026.[2]
Reviewed 2026-10-16 by Neo; information cutoff as in frontmatter.
US GAAP consolidated statements; fiscal year ends December 31; amounts in USD millions unless stated per share or in percent.[1]
**History covered:** 2016 and 2023-2025 annual statements and the six months to June 30, 2026.[1][2][7]

Diversification across species and 100-plus countries is the defense: international and livestock grew while US pets fell.[3]

### Final Verdict

Zoetis is a strong business with a wide but narrowing moat, adequate management whose recent buybacks wasted money, and resilient finances.[1][2]
At the NYSE close of $78.42 on 2026-10-15, Bear / Base / Bull cash-DCF values of $34 / $71 / $99 put the price 10.3% above Base and 21.1% below Bull (derived from the Valuation table; price from [8]).
My judgment is fairly valued relative to Base, with Medium confidence: the price already assumes a modest recovery, so there is no margin of safety.
US companion animal sales returning to growth would raise the Base case; a price near $50 would give a 30% margin.
