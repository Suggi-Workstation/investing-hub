# Alibaba: Cloud Growth Is Not Yet Owner Cash

**Research verdict: Investigate. Confidence: Low on value; stronger on reported facts.** The recovery thesis is plausible, but this review does not establish a cash-based margin of safety or recommend a trade.

**Identity and cutoff:** Alibaba Group Holding Limited; NYSE BABA ADS, each representing eight ordinary shares (HKEX 9988/89988); accounts in CNY under US GAAP; March year-end. History: FY2022-2026, June 2026 quarter, August share return and financing; information checked September 23, 2026.[1][2][7]

## Business and Method

| Question | Assessment |
|:--|:--|
| Who pays, and why? | Merchants buy access/marketing on Taobao/Tmall; cloud customers buy computing; international commerce and other businesses broaden the mix.[1] |
| Main economic drivers | Merchant monetization, commerce competitive spending, cloud utilization/pricing and the cash return on AI infrastructure, not AI revenue alone. |
| Industry and competence | Commerce platforms plus capital-intensive cloud; chip access, equipment life and AI investment returns are the decisive competence limits.[1][2] |
| Principal method / cross-check | Two-stage **earnings proxy**, not cash DCF; test it against actual cash after investment, no-growth value and funding needs. |

## Valuation

**Completed-company valuation: Not calculable from this evidence; value and margin of safety N/A.** The following explicitly conditional earnings scenarios are supplementary, not a substitute for resolving the cash and ownership gaps.

Bridge: FY2026 common profit 105.904 + common non-GAAP adjustments (-41.365) - full consolidated SBC 11.180 = 53.359; remove June-2025 equivalent 32.188 and add June-2026 equivalent 19.206 = **40.377**, rounded down to 40.[1][2] Full pre-tax consolidated SBC is a conservative proxy, not an exact minority/tax allocation; cash conversion remains unproved. Convertibles remain debt, without if-converted interest add-backs; award/conversion settlement is unresolved, so this is a provisional share-value screen, not fully diluted intrinsic value.
