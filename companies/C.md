# Citigroup -- A Recovering Bank Priced for Its Near-Term Plan

Citigroup is a global bank whose best business, Services, moves money and holds securities for multinational clients, and whose group return on tangible common equity (RoTCE) was 7.7% in 2025 and 12.6% in the first nine months of 2026.[1][4]
Its Investor Day targets are an RoTCE of about 11-13% in the near term and about 14-15% in the medium term.[10]
On the method used here the shares are worth about their price only if returns of roughly 11% last (derived).
A higher value needs the medium-term range to be reached and held; the highest RoTCE from 2021 to 2025 was 13.4%.[1]
**Thesis status:** Thesis not supported at the current price. **Confidence:** Medium. **Valuation status:** Completed; Base value $116 per share against a $121.97 price (excess returns on tangible book, not a cash DCF).

**Identity and basis:** Citigroup Inc. (Delaware), common stock C on the NYSE; one class of common stock; 1,655.2 million common shares outstanding at the end of the third quarter of 2026.[4]
Reviewed 2026-11-10 by Neo; information cutoff as in frontmatter.
US GAAP, consolidated, fiscal year ending December 31; USD millions unless stated.
**History covered:** fiscal 2015 and fiscal 2021 to 2025, and the first nine months of 2026.[1][2][7]

**Summary:** Citi earns money from five different businesses that share one balance sheet and one set of regulators.
The useful measure for the group is the return on tangible common equity, because regulators decide how much equity each dollar of business needs.
Services is the part an owner can understand and count on; Markets is the largest user of equity and the least visible.
The main accounting trap is treating one strong year for trading and credit as the normal level.

**Summary:** The supported defense is switching costs in Services: clients' daily payment flows and operating balances are hard to move.
It is a real advantage, but not a unique one, since client surveys rank two US rivals ahead of Citi at home and one ahead globally.
Outside Services no mechanism is established.

**Summary:** Buyer power and rivalry are the strongest pressures, and they bind hardest in cards, Markets and Banking.
Services offsets them with switching costs; the rest of the group does not.
That is why a year-to-date RoTCE of 29.7% in Services sits beside 12.6% for the group.[4][5]

## Valuation

**Coverage: Assessed.** Valuation date 2026-11-10; method: excess returns on tangible common equity at a 10% required return; USD per common share; basis: tangible book value per share of $101.59 and 1,655.2 million shares.[4]
Value per share = tangible book value x (sustained RoTCE - growth) / (10% - growth); each case holds its return and growth constant, and a second calculation from distributable earnings gives the same values (derived).
Owner earnings on this basis are normalized earnings to common shareholders: at the Base return about $11.17 a share, a 9.2% yield on the price, of which about $8.13 is distributable if equity must grow 3% a year with the balance sheet (derived).
Net income available to common shareholders over the last four quarters was $18,052 million, about $10.91 per share now outstanding (derived from [4]).
Year-to-date dividends and repurchases run at about 10.9% of market value a year, but they exceed earnings and draw on released capital, so they are not the sustainable coupon (derived from [2][4][15]).

|:--|:--|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| Base | 11% / 3% (assumption): the low end of the 2027-2028 target range | 6.99 | 7.52 | 116 | 81 | 58 |
| Bull | 14% / 4% (assumption): the low end of the 2029-2031 target range, held | 29.08 | 38.65 | 169 | 119 | 85 |
| Bear | 8% / 2% (assumption): near the 2022 to 2025 average of 7.1% | -13.46 | -11.94 | 76 | 53 | 38 |

**Summary:** Value moves almost entirely with the return Citi can sustain: each point of RoTCE is worth about $15 a share in the Base case (derived).
At a 9% required return the Base value is about $135, at 11% about $102, and if excess returns end after year ten about $109 (derived).
Marking the held-to-maturity securities to fair value would take about $6.60 off tangible book per share after tax (derived from [2][4][5]).
At $121.97 the price is 1.20 times tangible book and implies a sustained RoTCE of about 11.4% at a 10% required return and 3% growth (derived from [4][15]).
The price therefore asks for about what the 2027-2028 plan promises; only the Bull case, the full medium-term plan, leaves a discount.
Confidence is Medium: the arithmetic is simple, and the decisive input, the sustainable return, has one strong year behind it.

**Summary:** The weakest assumption is that 2026's returns are the new normal and not the top of a cycle.
The next decisive evidence is a full year of returns in ordinary markets with normal credit losses.
Evidence that All Other's losses and idle capital are shrinking as planned would strengthen the case; a return to single-digit returns would break it.

### Final Verdict

Citigroup combines one business with real switching costs, an adequate management team that has simplified the bank, and finances that deserve watching but not alarm.[4][5]
At $121.97 (NYSE close, November 9, 2026) the shares stand against Bear/Base/Bull estimated values of $76/$116/$169 per share, 5% above the Base value (derived from [15]).[15]
These are values from excess returns on tangible book, not intrinsic values from a cash DCF, so no margin of safety is claimed.
The judgment is approximately fairly valued, with Medium confidence: the price already pays for the 2027-2028 plan, and a discount exists only if the 14-15% range is reached and held.
The decisive risk is that returns fall back toward the 7.1% average of 2022 to 2025 when markets and credit turn (derived from [1]).
