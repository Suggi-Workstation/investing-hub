# Nintendo -- A Durable Franchise Priced for More Cash Than Its Best Year Produced

Nintendo sells games and the hardware to play them, and its characters sell both.
It owes nothing but lease obligations and holds very large liquid assets, but its cash earnings swing with each console generation.[1][2]
Owner earnings are about JPY 199 a share from operations and about JPY 273 with after-tax interest and the associates' profit counted, 2.3% and 3.2% of the share price (derived).
**Thesis status:** Thesis not supported at the current price. **Confidence:** Medium. **Valuation status:** Completed; Base value JPY 3,770 per share against a JPY 8,606 price (cash DCF for operations, associates at an earnings multiple).

**Identity and basis:** Nintendo Co., Ltd. (Japan); ordinary shares on the Tokyo exchange under code 7974.[13]
NTDOY is an unsponsored ADS, of which 4 represent one ordinary share.[12]
There were 1,287,260,000 shares including treasury shares and 134,341,050 treasury shares as of Sep. 30, 2026, which leaves 1,152,918,950 outstanding (derived from [2]).
Reviewed 2026-11-10 by Neo; information cutoff as in frontmatter.
Accounting principles generally accepted in Japan, consolidated; fiscal years end March 31, so fiscal 2026 ended March 31, 2026; filing figures in JPY millions as printed; valuation inputs in JPY billions.
**History covered:** fiscal 2013 to 2026.[1][10][11]
Also the six months ended September 30, 2026; the half-year release has no cash flow statement.[2]

## Business and Method

**Coverage: Assessed.**

| Question | Assessment / evidence |
|:--|:--|
| Who pays, for what, and why do customers return? | Consumers buy the hardware, the games and digital content because they want the characters. In fiscal 2026 Nintendo Switch 2 hardware reached 19.86 million units and Mario Kart World sold 14.70 million units.[1] |
| Segments, geography and principal competitors | Nintendo operates as a single business segment.[1] The proportion of sales outside Japan was 78.9% in the first half of fiscal 2027.[3] Rivals are other consoles, PC and phone games, and other entertainment. |
| Main revenue, margin and cash-generation drivers | Software sold into the installed base. In the six months ended September 30, 2026 net sales fell 12.1% to 966,041 and operating profit rose 49.1% to 216,500.[2] Switch 2 hardware fell 37.4% to 6.49 million units, Switch 2 software rose 18.8% to 24.49 million units and digital sales rose 60.5% to 249.6 bn yen.[3] |
| Capital required and who bears financing risk | Development and launch inventory, paid from its own cash. Research and development expenses were 177,890 in fiscal 2026, and inventories rose from 539,804 to 814,570 in the half to September 30, 2026.[1][2] |
| Sector-specific measures and historical comparison | Operating profit (loss) was (36,410) in fiscal 2013 and (46,425) in fiscal 2014.[11] It was 640,634 in fiscal 2021 and 360,117 in fiscal 2026.[1][10] |
| Circle of competence and decisive limitations | The model of a platform built on its own characters is understandable; the timing of hits is not. The filings do not show the cash economics of The Pokemon Company, an associate in which the percentage of voting rights held by Nintendo is 32.[1] |
| Principal valuation method / useful cross-check | Simple DCF on five-year average core operating cash, with liquid assets and associates valued separately. Cross-checks: operating profit after tax over ten years, and the cash the price requires. |

**Summary:** Nintendo earns most in the years after a successful launch, when software sells into a large installed base.
One year's profit is therefore a poor guide, in either direction.
The accounting trap is that profit includes the associates' share, interest and currency gains, none of which is operating cash.
The first half of fiscal 2027 also includes approximately 300 million U.S. dollars of IEEPA tariff refunds, recorded as a reduction of cost of sales.[3]

## Valuation

**Coverage: Assessed.** Valuation date 2026-11-10; simple DCF at a 10% required return; JPY billions.
**Valuation type: cash DCF for operations; associates at an earnings multiple.**
Core cash is operating cash flow less purchases of property, plant and equipment and intangible assets, less interest and dividends received after tax at the statutory tax rate of 30.5% (derived from [1]).
It was 278.7, 283.7, 409.3, -49.1 and 226.1 in fiscal 2022 to 2026 (derived from [1][4][5]).
Input line: Nintendo, 2026-11-10; core operating cash, five-year average; V0 229.7; A 896.3 before associates; S 1.152919 billion; P JPY 8,606 (derived).
A is 90% of liquid assets of 2,071.1, less a reserve of 750 to run the business through a failed generation, the retirement benefit liability of 18,275, non-controlling interests of 1,229, the lease obligations and 190.7 for the present value of the facilities plan (assumption; derived from [1][2]).
Liquid assets are cash, deposits and securities at September 30, 2026 plus investment securities at March 31, 2026 other than the 278,251 invested in unconsolidated subsidiaries and associates (derived from [1][2]).
Associates are added at an assumed share of profit of 35 / 60 / 100 in Bear / Base / Bull, at 8 / 10 / 12 times, less 20% for tax and access: 224 / 480 / 960 (assumption).
Their share of profit was 30,099 in fiscal 2024, 35,125 in fiscal 2025 and 82,792 in fiscal 2026.[1][6]
In the six months ended September 30, 2026 the share of profit of entities accounted for using equity method was 60,822 against 44,173 a year earlier.[2]
Owner earnings from operations: about JPY 199 a share, a 2.3% yield on the price and about 2.7% on the enterprise value left after liquid assets and associates (derived).
With after-tax interest income and the Base share of associates' profit: about JPY 273 a share, a 3.2% yield, close to the forecast profit per share of 268.90 yen for fiscal 2027 (derived from [2]).
On ten-year average operating profit after tax the operating figure is about JPY 224 a share (derived from [1][10]).

### Final Verdict

Nintendo is a good business with a narrow moat around its characters, adequate managers and no debt, whose cash earnings are cyclical.[1][2]
At JPY 8,606 per share (Tokyo close, November 9, 2026) the price stands against Bear/Base/Bull estimated values of JPY 2,288/JPY 3,770/JPY 5,559, 128% above the Base value (derived from [13]).[13]
These are cash DCF values for the operations with the associates at an earnings multiple; the liquid assets are counted in full less a reserve.
The judgment is overvalued relative to Base, with Medium confidence: even the generous case stays below the price.
This is a research assessment of an existing holding, not a sell instruction; a good business is not a bargain at any price.
