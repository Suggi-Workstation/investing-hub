# Alphabet - Management, Moat and DCF v1

**Management: 3.0/5 (adequate/mixed). Moat: 3.5/5 (moderate forward rating). Valuation confidence: low.**

Research completed 2026-09-08. **Historical test snapshot: July 23, 2026**, using FY2020-FY2025 and the June 30, 2026 interim statements released July 22.[14][15][16]

GOOGL Class A reference close: **$317.69 on July 23**, not a current quote.[12]

The August 6 prospectus filings and August 10 8-K were identified but not fully retrievable: direct SEC access returned 403 and extracted bodies were incomplete. Consequently, this is **not a reconciled September valuation**. Later financing, cash deployment and share changes require an update before investment use. This cutoff is a limitation, not a claim that no later events occurred.

## Business and evidence

Advertisers pay to reach intent through Search and audiences through YouTube; enterprise customers pay for Cloud infrastructure, software and collaboration services. These are different markets. Q2 Search & other revenue was $63.271bn, Cloud revenue $24.768bn, and total revenue $119.796bn. Services and Cloud reported operating income of $39.544bn and $8.814bn, before unallocated Alphabet-level costs.[14][16]

Best counter-case to the cautious allocation rating: the infrastructure may secure scarce capacity and sustain Cloud growth. Reassess upward on durable **after-investment cash growth per share**, not merely utilization, revenue or stock appreciation. Founder holdings are dated April; the proxy excludes non-voting Class C from its ownership table, so it cannot establish each executive's complete economic exposure.[11][17]

The filing also reports $10.0bn of net proceeds from a June private placement to a Berkshire Hathaway affiliate.[13] That is evidence of committed capital, not proof that Buffett personally made the decision or that intrinsic value equals the issue price.

## Moat scorecard

**3.5/5 | Moderate/narrow forward candidate; powerful existing franchise | Trend: mixed | Confidence: medium.** A defensible working horizon is about five years, with low confidence beyond it; this is a judgment, not a promised moat expiry date. Historical reach and profitability support an advantage, but inference costs and alternative interfaces may change who captures the value.

### Porter: higher means worse competitive pressure

Global Search/YouTube advertising and Cloud are assessed separately. Ratings are analyst judgments informed by the filings and search judgment, not measured Porter statistics.[7][14][17]

| Force | Search/YouTube pressure /5 | Cloud pressure /5 | Mechanism and impact |
|:--|--:|--:|:--|
| New entrants | 3 | 4 | Scale and distribution impede entrants; well-funded AI developers can nevertheless attack the interface or infrastructure layer. |
| Supplier power | 4 | 4 | Distribution partners, chips, energy and capacity absorb economics. Q2 TAC was $16.179bn; Cloud plans third-party capacity that management says pressures near-term margins.[16][17] |
| Buyer power | 2 | 3 | Advertisers are fragmented but can move budgets; large enterprise customers can negotiate and use multiple providers. No customer-level bargaining dataset was obtained. |
| Substitutes | 4 | 3 | AI answers can satisfy intent without a search click; on-premise or alternative infrastructure can replace some Cloud workloads. |
| Rivalry | 4 | 5 | Competing ad channels and AI interfaces contest attention; Cloud investment and product competition remain intense. |

**Binding threat:** AI substitutes for Search, combined with capital-intensive rivalry in Cloud. The December 5, 2025 final search judgment restricts specified tying/default-distribution arrangements. Do not confuse a final order with proof of its ultimate competitive effect; appeals and implementation remain monitoring items.[7]

## DCF assumptions and reconciliation

**Illustrative FCFF, not reported owner earnings.** The maintenance/growth capex split was not verified. FY2025 cash FCF less the CFO SBC addback is $48.313bn; the TTM equivalent is $25.126bn. These are cash/SBC diagnostics, not FCFF or a maintenance-adjusted owner-earnings estimate. FY2025 total P&L SBC was approximately $27.1bn, distinct from the $24.953bn CFO addback.[13][14][16]

Forecast FCFF = EBIT*(1-tax) + D&A - **total** capex - incremental operating NWC. EBIT margins retain SBC, R&D and ordinary operating costs. The $99.031bn Q2 equity-security gain is excluded from operating forecasts; related financial assets are valued separately. Prior accrued fines are deducted in the bridge rather than forecast again as a new charge.[13][16]

**Timing:** Y1 is the next full annual period ending July 23, 2027; Y10 ends July 23, 2036. No already-earned fiscal year is discounted as wholly future cash. The June TTM revenue anchor is $445.866bn; Y1's uplift is an estimate for that prospective period, not a reported year-on-year growth rate.

| Assumption | Bear | Base | Bull |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Business outcome | AI/remedies impair pricing; costly buildout earns poor returns | Search adapts; Cloud grows; capital efficiency gradually recovers | AI monetization and Cloud utilization justify the buildout |
| Y1 revenue uplift / Y10 growth | 10% / 2% | 16% / 4% | 25% / 5% |
| EBIT margin Y1 / Y10 | 30% / 22% | 33% / 30% | 35% / 36% |
| Y1 total capex | $235bn | $225bn | $245bn |
| Y10 capex / D&A as % sales | 18% / 12% | 14% / 12% | 14% / 12% |
| Normalized tax rate | 20% | 20% | 20% |
| Incremental NWC / revenue increase | 5% | 5% | 5% |
| Nominal USD unlevered return hurdle | 10% | 10% | 10% |
| Terminal growth | 2% | 2.5% | 3% |

All forecast inputs are **Neo assumptions**, not guidance. The July call guided FY2026 capex to $195-205bn, above the previous $180-190bn range, and expected another significant increase in 2027 without a quantified range. Our rolling Y1 is a different period. D&A rises with the asset base; it is a scenario assumption, not a verified asset-vintage depreciation model.[17]

The 10% hurdle is an opportunity-cost requirement on unlevered USD cash flows, not a claim about Buffett's universal rate or a measured CAPM/WACC. The 8-12% sensitivity below shows the rate dependence. Terminal FCFF grows from Y10 while retaining its reinvestment burden; no terminal cash-flow boost is taken merely because growth slows.

Portfolio values are held constant to isolate operating scenarios; this understates joint operating/portfolio downside risk. If the entire modeled investment portfolio were worth zero, each positive scenario value would fall by approximately $9.93/share. SpaceX sale restrictions and the concentration of private investments make carrying values unsuitable as cash. No separate Waymo premium is added on top of consolidated operations.[13]

**Shares: 12.426458bn pro forma.** Start with 12.230bn actual common shares, add 142m treasury-stock-method award equivalents as an explicit weighted-average proxy, and add 54.458250m maximum preferred-conversion equivalents. The Q2 diluted EPS figure of 12.309bn includes only 16m time-weighted preferred equivalents and is not a point-in-time capitalization.[13]

Preferred calculation: 385m depositary shares / 20 = 19.25m preferred shares; half in each series. Maximum conversion uses 2.8160 Class A and 2.8420 Class C shares per preferred share. No capped-call benefit is assumed. Remaining dividends are reserved as 19.25m*$12.15 plus 11 regular quarterly payments at 6.25% on $1,000. **Do not also deduct the $18.023bn preferred book value or liquidation preference.** This is a conservative as-if-converted approximation, not an option valuation; special conversion/anti-dilution events are not modeled.[13]

The award proxy is not a grant-by-grant cap table. Ongoing SBC remains charged in operating margins; no extra future SBC dilution or buyback-driven share shrinkage is forecast. Operating-lease rents remain in costs, so $18.037bn of operating-lease obligations are not deducted again. Future capacity commitments must be funded through the modeled total capex; guarantee losses beyond it remain a risk, not an assumed zero exposure.[13]

### What would change the assessment?

- **Improve:** cash returns per share rise after the infrastructure buildout, and Cloud economics remain attractive after shared AI costs. Rebuild the capital bridge first.
- **Impair:** commercial query monetization deteriorates, required capex fails to normalize, or material remedies/guarantee losses absorb more owner cash.
- **Still missing:** verified maintenance capex, asset-vintage depreciation, a full award/convertible and minority-interest valuation, peer operating ROIC, and reconciled post-July financing. Until resolved, this remains a low-confidence learning model, not an actionable bargain claim.

## Verification and reuse

All six historical years and 42 requested data cells were matched to independently downloaded issuer PDFs; statement values and share aggregation were checked, and TTM FCF was reconciled to the release. The three scenarios were actually run through the skill's CLI and independently recalculated. Score weights, MoS prices, sensitivity and cash-flow bridges were computed, not estimated mentally. Calculator regression tests passed after rejecting malformed currency identifiers.

Data-quality exception: the extracted July transcript says "$87.1 million" of marketable equity; the 10-Q reports $87.063bn. The balance-sheet figures take precedence, and that transcript unit discrepancy is not propagated.[13][17]

Framework distinction: this note uses Neo's new evidence-led rubrics and explicit FCFF hurdle rather than silently blending legacy CAPM, ownership cutoffs or mechanical scoring rules. Existing framework files were left unchanged. Methodology follows reinvestment-aware cash valuation, not an assertion that Buffett/Munger use these scores or assumptions.[1][2]
