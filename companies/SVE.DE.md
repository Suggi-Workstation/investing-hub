# Shareholder Value Beteiligungen: A NAV Discount Needs a Cash Exit

**Research verdict: Investigate. Confidence: Low.** The wrapper trades below reported portfolio NAV, but investment selection, restricted holdings, ongoing costs and minority owners' ability to capture the discount remain decisive.

**Identity and cutoff:** Shareholder Value Beteiligungen AG, not Shareholder Value Management AG; SVE.DE/Xetra, ISIN DE000A168205, EUR, calendar year.[3][7]
Evidence: FY2021-2025 reported return history, audited HGB FY2025 accounts, unaudited June 2026 interim, August 31 NAV/holdings and September 23 share quote.[2][3][14]
No portfolio changes or trades are proposed.

## Valuation

**Operating-company DCF: N/A. Independent intrinsic value and intrinsic margin of safety: Not calculable from this evidence.** The appropriate completed calculation is an **adjusted quoted-price NAV sensitivity**, not a claim that market prices equal business values.

August NAV EUR119.76 x **677,029 outside shares** implies EUR81.081m, assuming the latest verified June count remained unchanged; denominator reconciles 697,500 issued less 20,471 treasury shares.[3][5]
NAV already subtracts current liabilities/provisions: no second deduction, and no addition of the anticipated EUR7m realization proceeds.[5][7]

Model partitions NAV into 27.6% Armira/Acceleratio, EUR2m banks/receivables assumed recoverable, and the residual investment proxy.[5][13] Deduct flat future parent costs as a perpetuity at the fixed 10% hurdle; distinguish future costs from those already incurred. Additional realization reserves cover only unprovided future leakage, not liabilities already in NAV. No extra blanket holding-company discount.

| Scenario | Restricted / other-investment markdown | Annual parent cost | Extra realization reserve | Adjusted quoted NAV/share |
|:--|:--|--:|--:|--:|
| Base | 20% / 0% | EUR1.0m | 1% of NAV | **EUR97.18** |
| Bull | 0% / 0% | EUR0.8m | 0% | **EUR107.94** |
| Bear | 40% / 30% | EUR1.2m | 2% of NAV | **EUR61.29** |

These are explicit analyst assumptions, not independently verified recoveries or a guaranteed range. No market appreciation is assumed. Each EUR100k of additional annual overhead costs EUR1.48/share; another ten-point markdown on the restricted sleeve costs EUR3.31/share. Applying all 60,000 authorized rights immediately with zero exercise proceeds gives Base EUR89.27, **only a conservative denominator sensitivity**, not actual grant dilution or contractual settlement.[19]

**Price last:** Xetra quote EUR80.00, September 23 at 15:35:28 UTC.[18] This is 33.2% below the **older August NAV**, not a contemporaneous measured discount; the matched August 31 price EUR74 implied 38.2%.[5] Price is 17.7% below the conditional Base NAV and above Bear: none of these spreads establishes an intrinsic margin of safety.
