# Lululemon -- A Cash-Rich Brand in a Turnaround, Priced as if the Turnaround Fails

Lululemon sells premium athletic apparel through about 825 company-operated stores and online, and revenue grew from $2,060,523 thousand in fiscal 2015 to $11,102,600 thousand in fiscal 2025.[1][2][6]
In 2026 the business turned down: second-quarter comparable sales fell 9%, Americas comparable sales fell 12%, and full-year revenue is now expected to decline 5% to 7%.[2]
The CEO left, founder Chip Wilson ran a proxy fight that ended in a cooperation agreement, and Heidi O'Neill became CEO on September 8, 2026.[4][7][8]
**Thesis status:** Thesis supported with a modest margin. **Confidence:** Low. **Valuation status:** Completed; Base value $127 per share against a $99.09 price.

**Identity and basis:** lululemon athletica inc. (Delaware), common stock LULU on Nasdaq; 105,594,064 common shares plus 5,115,961 exchangeable shares (economically equivalent) at August 28, 2026, 110,710,025 in total (derived).[3]
Reviewed 2026-10-16 by Neo; information cutoff as in frontmatter.
US GAAP consolidated statements; fiscal year ends on the Sunday closest to January 31; fiscal 2025 ended February 1, 2026; amounts in USD thousands in quoted statements and USD millions in derived figures unless stated per share or in percent.[1]
**History covered:** fiscal 2015 and fiscal 2023-2025 annual statements and the two quarters to August 2, 2026.[1][3][6]

### Final Verdict

Lululemon is a profitable premium brand with a weak and narrowing moat, weak management in transition, and a resilient debt-free balance sheet.[1][2][3]
At the Nasdaq close of $99.09 on 2026-10-15, Bear / Base / Bull earnings-proxy values of $82 / $127 / $175 put the price 22.1% below Base and 20.4% above Bear (derived from the Valuation table; price from [13]).
My judgment is undervalued against Base but with Low confidence: the price discounts continued decline, yet the brand's durability cannot be judged from the evidence.
Americas comparable sales returning to growth would support the Base case; another guidance cut would invalidate it.
